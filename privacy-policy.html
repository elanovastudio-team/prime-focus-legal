Section 01

Introduction

Welcome to Prime Focus (Package: com.etla.primefocus, Version 1.1.7), an Android productivity application developed by Elanova Studios ("we", "us", or "our"). We prioritize your privacy and are dedicated to protecting your personal information.

Prime Focus is engineered around an offline-first architecture. Your productivity records, focus logs, and timer settings are processed and stored locally on your device, allowing core features to run seamlessly without transmitting your personal productivity data to developer servers.

By installing, accessing, or using Prime Focus, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Section 02

Offline-First Architecture & Network Usage

Prime Focus operates with a 100% locally stored database (using Android Room DB and DataStore Preferences). Your focus session logs, custom Collections, scheduled routines, Time Laps breakdowns, and app settings are saved directly on your physical Android device.

While core productivity timing functions operate completely without an active internet connection, Prime Focus utilizes declared network permissions (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) exclusively for two essential background operations:

  • Validating digital licenses and processing Premium Purchases via Google Play Billing (v8.0.0).
  • Fetching and rendering in-app advertisements via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob SDK 25.4.0) in the app's free tier.
Section 03

Information We Do NOT Collect

Elanova Studios does NOT operate custom remote application servers, user registration systems, or database backends to harvest your personal data. We do NOT collect or store:

  • Email addresses, user names, or registration profiles
  • Passwords or account authentication credentials
  • Personal productivity session logs or custom Collection names
  • Cloud backups or remote server productivity archives
  • Device contacts, address book data, or social media profiles
  • Photos, image galleries, media files, or personal documents
  • Personal text messages, SMS, or chat logs
Section 04

Third-Party Services & Advertising (AdMob)

To support the ongoing development of Prime Focus, the free version integrates **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob SDK 25.4.0)** to display banner, interstitial, or rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain device information and telemetry to serve relevant ads, prevent ad fraud, and evaluate ad performance. This technical data includes:

  • Device Identifiers: Such as the Google Advertising ID (AAID).
  • Network Information: IP address, mobile network state, and generalized location data.
  • Usage Diagnostics: Ad impression data, user interaction metrics, and crash reporting logs.

For more detailed information regarding Google's data privacy practices, please review Google's Privacy & Terms for Advertising.

Users who purchase a Premium Subscription or Lifetime Upgrade enjoy a completely ad-free experience, during which ad requests are disabled.

Section 05

Local Data & Storage

All productivity information managed within Prime Focus resides strictly on your physical Android device via Room DB and DataStore Preferences:

  • Productivity Records: Focus session duration logs, timer histories, custom Collection labels, scheduled routines, and Time Laps metrics are stored locally on your device.
  • Preferences & Settings: App configuration parameters, notification preferences, sound choices, and theme selections are preserved locally.
  • Local Operational Metrics: Performance metrics and feature usage counters are maintained locally on your device to maintain app stability without sending usage telemetry to developer servers.
Section 06

Subscriptions & Google Play Billing

Prime Focus offers optional paid Premium Subscriptions (Monthly or Annual) and a one-time Lifetime Purchase tier to unlock advanced productivity features and remove advertisements.

Premium purchases are processed and managed through Google Play Billing (v8.0.0). All financial transactions, recurring renewals, cancellations, and refund requests are governed directly by Google Play policies. Elanova Studios does not collect, process, or store credit card credentials or financial payment details.

Section 07

Device Permissions & System Features

Prime Focus requests specific Android system permissions strictly to support app functionality:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to display focus timer alerts, session completion reminders, and active timer notifications.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to trigger scheduled focus routines and break alarms at precise user-configured times (Target SDK 36 / Android 15).
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): Required to maintain active timer execution continuously when the app is minimized or the screen is turned off.
  • Network Access (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to validate Google Play Billing purchase licenses and load AdMob advertising assets.
Section 08

Data Retention, Clearing & Uninstalling

All productivity data stays on your physical device:

  • Uninstalling the Application: Uninstalling Prime Focus permanently removes locally stored database records from your device.
  • Clearing Android Storage: Clearing app storage via Android system settings permanently resets locally stored information.
  • No Cloud Data Recovery: Elanova Studios cannot recover deleted local data because we do not operate cloud backup servers.
Section 09

Children's Privacy

Prime Focus is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age (or applicable age in your jurisdiction).

Because Prime Focus requires no user account registration, no personal information from children is collected or maintained.

Section 10

Your Privacy & Ad Choices

You maintain full control over your device permissions and advertising choices:

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID (AAID) at any time through your Android system settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID.
  • Notification Controls: You can enable or disable notification permissions at any time via Android app settings (Settings > Apps > Prime Focus > Notifications).
  • Local Data Control: You can clear local app data or uninstall the app to remove all locally saved records.
